### Welcome to Ultramarine for Chromebook!

With the help from out friends over at the [Chrultrabook Project](https://docs.chrultrabook.com/), we built a no-hassle way to boot full Ultramarine onto your Chromebook!

These pages will get you everything you need to know about what Ultramarine for Chromebook is, how it works, how you can install it, and even how you can help us out.
